# Code of Conduct

The Tyr Project is committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, and technically focused community.

---

## Our Standards

We encourage:

- Respectful discussion
- Constructive feedback
- Technical curiosity
- Collaboration
- Professional conduct

We do not tolerate:

- Personal attacks
- Harassment
- Discrimination
- Trolling
- Deliberate disruption of discussions

---

## Technical Discussions

Disagreement is expected.

Language design and compiler architecture often involve differing opinions.

Debate ideas.

Do not attack people.

---

## Contributions

All contributors are welcome regardless of experience level.

Questions are encouraged.

Constructive criticism is encouraged.

Learning is encouraged.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Repeated or severe violations may result in removal from project spaces.

---

## Our Goal

The Tyr Project exists to build great software together.

Every contributor deserves to feel respected while doing so.
